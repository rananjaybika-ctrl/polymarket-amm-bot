## Bugs Present in This Data

### 1. Z-Score Calculation Bug
- **Location:** `src/api/binance_client.py:359-379`
- **Issue:** Dividing total % change by tick-to-tick std dev
- **Effect:** Z-scores of 100-12,000 instead of -3 to +3
- **Impact:** Excessive BULLISH bias detection

### 2. Winner Conflict Bug
- **Location:** `scripts/run_paper_bot.py`
- **Issue:** Separate processes set different strike prices
- **Effect:** 46% of markets resolved differently than Standard/Opportunistic
- **Impact:** Wrong PNL calculations for 35 markets
