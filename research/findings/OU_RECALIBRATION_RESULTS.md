# OU Recalibration Results

**Date:** February 7, 2026

## Problem Statement

The original OU parameters were calibrated on 60-second intervals from only 1,550 samples (Jan 18+ data). This caused:
- All z-scores falling into LOW regime (99.5%)
- FOLLOW signal broken at 54% accuracy (coin flip)
- Regime filter non-functional

## Data Used

**Source:** `research/observer/PROTECTED_grid_obs_is_oos2_combined.csv`
- 1,090,500 rows of 5Hz observer data
- `binance_price` column extracted
- Duration: 69.4 hours (Jan 17-19, 2026)
- Price range: $91,910 - $95,639

## Diagnosis

### Old (60s) - BROKEN
- **Problem:** sigma_stat too small (0.39) because calibrated on smoothed 60s data
- **Effect:** Real 1s volatility appears as HIGH z-scores but params expect LOW
- **Result:** All z-scores negative, regime filter useless

### New (1s) - GOOD
- **Fix:** sigma_stat = 1.68, properly captures 1s return variance
- **Result:** ~38% LOW, ~61% MEDIUM, ~1.5% HIGH - reasonable distribution
- **Mean z-score:** -0.01 (centered correctly)

### New (5Hz) - BEST
- **Fix:** sigma_stat = 2.84, matches native 5Hz data frequency
- **Result:** ~8% LOW, ~85% MEDIUM, ~7% HIGH - excellent distribution
- **Mean z-score:** +0.87 (slightly above mean due to volatile period)

## Recommendation

**Use 1s calibration for now:**
- File: `research/ou_params_1s.json`
- Reason: Better match to typical trading decisions (made on ~1s timeframes)
- The 5Hz params have more noise but could be useful for sub-second analysis
